# Master Planner customers, sites and work orders

Customers, Sites and Work orders appear after the calendar entries in the sidebar.

## Customers

The customer list displays name, main phone, optional email, and the number of active sites. Search matches name, either phone, and email without case sensitivity. Active customers appear by default; **Show archived** switches to archived customers only. The search and archive filter are local screen state.

**Add customer** opens the same form used for editing. Name and phone are required and cannot contain only whitespace. All fields are trimmed; phone formatting otherwise remains as entered. Additional phone, email, and notes are optional. Saving a new customer opens their card.

The card displays contact details, clickable phone and email links, notes, and the customer's sites. Active and archived sites can be viewed separately. **Add site** preselects this customer. Archiving requires confirmation and returns to the customer list. It keeps the customer's record and does not archive or delete any sites. An archived card shows its status and allows editing, but does not offer archiving again or adding a site.

## Sites

The site list displays name, address, and customer name. Search matches all three without case sensitivity. Active sites appear by default; **Show archived** switches to archived sites only.

**Add site** requires an existing active customer, site name, and address. Notes are optional. The same form edits existing sites. Text fields are trimmed. An existing site belonging to an archived customer keeps that owner available as a disabled option, so its name, address, and notes can still be edited; archived customers cannot be assigned to a new site.

The site card links to its customer's card and phone number. Archiving requires confirmation, keeps the record, and returns to the site list.

## Saving

All CRM forms use the existing Master Planner CRM persistence service. While a save is pending, the form remains open with editing disabled; it closes when the saved record appears in the reactive state. A failed save leaves the form open for retry. Customer, site, and work order records remain available after restarting the application with the same profile.

## Work orders

The work order list shows title, customer, optional site, date and time, status, and optional work price. Search matches title, description, customer name, and site name. The status filter offers All, Planned, In progress, Waiting, Completed, and Cancelled. Active and archived records are shown separately.

The shared create/edit form requires an active customer and a nonblank title. A site is optional and belongs to the selected customer; changing the customer clears a site belonging to someone else. Existing archived customer/site links remain visible as disabled options when editing. Description, date, start/end times, price, and notes are optional. Work price must be a finite nonnegative number; it is displayed using the configured locale without a currency field.

Saving a new work order opens its card. The card links to the customer, optional site, and phone. Quick actions start work, mark it waiting, complete it, or cancel it. Completion records the current timestamp in completedAt; editing a completed order keeps that timestamp, and changing to another status removes it. Completion and cancellation do not archive records. Archiving requires confirmation, retains the record, and removes it from the calendar.

Customer and site cards list their nonarchived work orders. **Add work order** preselects the customer, or both customer and site when opened from a site card. Creating work for archived customer/site cards is not offered.

## Date and time storage

scheduledDay is an optional validated Gregorian date in YYYY-MM-DD form. scheduledStart and scheduledEnd are integer wall-clock minutes from local midnight on that date, not epoch timestamps. For example, 09:30 is 570 and 17:00 is 1020. Start is 0–1439; end is 1–1440. An end of 1440 displays as 24:00 and means the end of the scheduled day. When both are set, end must be greater than start. Overnight intervals are not supported. Time inputs use HH:mm; 24:00 is accepted only for the end. Times are independent of timezone offsets and daylight saving changes; no date means the order is unscheduled and does not appear in the calendar.

## Calendar appointments

Today, Tomorrow and Week display work orders separately from tasks. Within a day, timed orders are sorted by start time and untimed orders come last. Month cells show a separate work count and at most two titles; Year mini-months show an outlined work indicator. Work order cards in day views link to their detail pages. Month/year date links open the corresponding day.

Archived and cancelled work orders are excluded from the calendar. Completed orders remain on their scheduled day with a muted status. Editing the date updates the calendar reactively. Calendar composition reads the CRM service alongside Planner data; work orders do not become tasks and task drag/drop remains unchanged.

## Payments, materials and the work order balance

Work order cards contain **Financial summary**, **Payments** and **Materials**. These sections belong to the selected work order; customer and site cards do not aggregate financial amounts.

**Add payment** and **Edit payment** use one form with amount, date, optional method and notes. New payments require a finite positive amount. The default date is the current local calendar date, independent of the configured start of the planning day. Existing negative payments (refunds) remain visible and editable with negative amounts. Payments are sorted by date from earliest to latest. **Delete payment** requires confirmation and removes only that payment.

**Add material** and **Edit material** use one form with a nonblank name, finite positive quantity, optional unit, finite nonnegative unit price and notes. Unit is a free-text value. Each position displays its quantity, unit price and line total (quantity multiplied by unit price). **Delete material** requires confirmation and removes only that position.

The summary displays the work price, paid amount, remaining balance and materials total using the configured number locale without a currency field. Paid is the sum of all payments, including negative refunds. Materials total is the sum of the line totals. Remaining is work price minus paid; an unknown work price displays **Not set**. Overpayment displays **Remaining: 0** and a separate **Overpaid** amount, while the underlying balance calculation keeps its negative value. Materials do not change the work price or remaining balance. All totals are computed from current records, not stored.

The lists and summary update automatically after saving or deletion and persist through restart using the existing CRM storage. Completed work orders still allow financial changes. Archived work orders retain the payments, materials and summary as read-only history, with editing, adding and deletion unavailable.

General financial dashboards, task linking and external calendar integrations are not part of these screens.
