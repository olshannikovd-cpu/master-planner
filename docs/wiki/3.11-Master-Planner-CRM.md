# Master Planner customers and sites

Customers and Sites appear after the calendar entries in the sidebar.

## Customers

The customer list displays name, main phone, optional email, and the number of active sites. Search matches name, either phone, and email without case sensitivity. Active customers appear by default; **Show archived** switches to archived customers only. The search and archive filter are local screen state.

**Add customer** opens the same form used for editing. Name and phone are required and cannot contain only whitespace. All fields are trimmed; phone formatting otherwise remains as entered. Additional phone, email, and notes are optional. Saving a new customer opens their card.

The card displays contact details, clickable phone and email links, notes, and the customer's sites. Active and archived sites can be viewed separately. **Add site** preselects this customer. Archiving requires confirmation and returns to the customer list. It keeps the customer's record and does not archive or delete any sites. An archived card shows its status and allows editing, but does not offer archiving again or adding a site.

## Sites

The site list displays name, address, and customer name. Search matches all three without case sensitivity. Active sites appear by default; **Show archived** switches to archived sites only.

**Add site** requires an existing active customer, site name, and address. Notes are optional. The same form edits existing sites. Text fields are trimmed. An existing site belonging to an archived customer keeps that owner available as a disabled option, so its name, address, and notes can still be edited; archived customers cannot be assigned to a new site.

The site card links to its customer's card and phone number. Archiving requires confirmation, keeps the record, and returns to the site list.

## Saving

Both forms use the existing Master Planner CRM persistence service. While a save is pending, the form remains open with editing disabled; it closes when the saved record appears in the reactive state. A failed save leaves the form open for retry. Customer and site records remain available after restarting the application with the same profile.

Work orders, payments, materials, task linking, and calendar integration are not part of these screens.
